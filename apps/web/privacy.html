What is shared.

Updated November 15, 2026

This describes the current Lu2 prototype. Local cooking features and the online community handle different kinds of information.

On your phone

Guest pantry quantities, locally saved recipes, local drafts, cooking-session progress, and timer state are stored on the device. Downloaded media is stored in the app’s private files. The community integration does not upload your guest pantry or unrelated local drafts when you sign in.

Local data is not a promise of cloud backup or recovery. Removing app data or uninstalling can remove device-held content. Device backup behavior depends on the operating system and app configuration.

When you use the community

The community backend uses Supabase for authentication and data storage. Signing in sends the information needed for your selected sign-in method. Account records include an account identifier and email address. If a sign-in provider is used, the provider and Supabase process that sign-in; provider-supplied identity details may form part of the account.

Profile records can include a display name, username, avatar URL, biography, dietary or measurement preferences, and skill level where those features are used. Do not put private information in a public profile or recipe.

Submitting a recipe sends its title, description, ingredients, equipment, steps, media references, author identifier, and related publication data. The backend stores recipe revisions and automated moderation flags. Reviews send a rating, review text, and account identifier. Reports send the reported recipe, reason, and reporter identifier. Moderation decisions and related logs are also stored.

Who can see community information

Published recipes and published reviews are intended for public viewing. Profile information may be publicly readable. Account email is used for authentication and is not intentionally included in public recipe or review responses. Submission, report, and moderation access is controlled by backend permissions; authorized moderation and service operations process this information.

Public content can be downloaded by other users. Removing or archiving it on the service does not automatically erase copies already saved elsewhere.

Technical information and service providers

Online requests reach Supabase and its infrastructure. Technical request information, such as IP addresses, request times, and device or browser details, may be processed for service operation and security. The project has not established a published schedule for backend, authentication, or infrastructure-log retention.

When this website is hosted on Vercel, Vercel receives web requests and associated technical information to serve the pages. This static site adds no advertising, analytics scripts, account forms, or tracking cookies. Opening GitHub support or a sign-in provider takes you to a separate service with its own data practices.

Account deletion and privacy questions

There is no self-service account-deletion control available in this prototype. See account-deletion information for the current limitation and how to ask for a private contact method. Do not send identifying data through the public issue tracker.

A retention schedule, private privacy-contact channel, and complete account-deletion workflow have not yet been published. This page does not claim a deletion deadline or compliance certification.

Changes

This notice should be updated when the app’s data handling changes. Check the date above when reviewing the current prototype.
