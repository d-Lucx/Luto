Lu2 is an Android prototype. Features and content are still being developed, and errors or interruptions can occur.

Recipe guidance has limits

Automated content checks look for certain problems; they do not establish that a recipe was kitchen-tested, nutritionally verified, safe for a particular allergy, or reviewed by a cooking professional. A published community recipe is not automatically curated. The original draft recipe collection is not live.

Use your judgment about ingredients, allergies, food handling, equipment, and cooking times. Do not rely on a timer notification as your only safety check. Missing nutrition or media should be treated as unavailable information.

What you share

Submit only content you created or have permission to share, including photos and video. Do not submit private information, abusive material, misleading claims, or dangerous instructions. Keep reviews relevant and use reporting tools for content concerns.

Community submissions may be published after automated checks, held for review, or archived. Publication is not an endorsement. Public content may be downloaded by others; later removal cannot recall every saved copy.

Your device and account

Keep your own copies of anything you need to preserve. Device-local data is not a cloud backup. Protect sign-in codes and links, and do not share access to your account. Guest pantry data stays separate from community publishing.

Support and changes

This site does not sell a subscription or accept payment. No support response time or uninterrupted availability is promised. For bugs or questions, use Support. Read the privacy information and account-deletion limitations before creating a community account.
