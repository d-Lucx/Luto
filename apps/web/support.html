HELP & FEEDBACK

A place to ask.

Found a problem, or unsure how something works? Lu2’s support channel is the project’s GitHub issue tracker.

Open the issue tracker ↗

Public means public.

Do not post passwords, sign-in links, verification codes, access tokens, email addresses, or other private information in an issue. Check screenshots before sharing them. For a private account matter, ask for a private contact method without including account details.

What helps with a bug report

  • Your phone model, Android version, and Lu2 app version.
  • The steps that led to the problem and what you expected to happen.
  • Whether you were offline, plus the exact error message with private information removed.

Saved recipes and downloads

Saving keeps recipe text on your device. Photos and clips have a separate download state and need trustworthy verification metadata. If media is unavailable, text instructions remain the fallback. A remote image appearing while connected does not prove it was downloaded.

Accounts and private information

Signing out does not delete your account. Removing the app does not delete community submissions. Read the account-deletion information before sharing identifying details.

This prototype does not offer a staffed support desk or a guaranteed response time.
