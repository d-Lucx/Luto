ACCOUNT & DATA

Account deletion.

Updated November 15, 2026

The prototype does not currently have a self-service account-deletion screen or a private deletion-request form.

Do not post your account details.

The support tracker is public. Never post your email address, password, sign-in link, verification code, access token, or identity documents there.

How to raise a private request

You can open a GitHub issue asking the maintainer to provide a private contact method for an account-deletion request. Include no identifying account information. A private channel and a way to verify account ownership must be established before account details are exchanged or a deletion is performed.

Ask for a private contact method ↗

This is an information page, not a submitted deletion request. The project has not published a deletion timeline or a complete process for handling associated submissions, reports, logs, and retained copies.

Local removal is different

Signing out ends local account access; it does not delete the backend account. Uninstalling or clearing app data can remove local pantry, drafts, cooking progress, and downloads, but does not delete community account records or published submissions. Removing an offline download only removes that device’s copy.

Public recipes and reviews may already have been saved by others. A service-side deletion cannot automatically recall those copies.

Read Privacy for the current data categories and known limitations.
